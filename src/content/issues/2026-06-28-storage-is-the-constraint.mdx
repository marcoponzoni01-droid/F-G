Every winter produces a run of stories about the gas price. Almost none of them
are about the thing that decides whether the winter matters.

Gas is not consumed from production; it is consumed from storage, refilled across
the summer and drawn down across the winter. The spot price is a claim on the
marginal molecule today. The storage level is a claim on how many molecules exist
between now and March, and it is the second one that determines whether a cold
February is an inconvenience or a crisis.

## What moved

Very little, which is the point. The front-month contract traded in a narrow
range all summer while the injection season ran roughly nine percentage points
behind its five-year norm.

A quiet price alongside a deteriorating inventory path is not a contradiction.
Injection demand is price-inelastic in the short run — storage operators refill
because they are obliged to, not because the price is attractive — so a shortfall
shows up as a quantity first and only becomes a price once the shortfall is large
enough to threaten the withdrawal season.

## Why it moved

The transmission from inventory to interest rates is short, and shorter than most
people expect, because European gas sits directly in the consumer price index
rather than at the end of a long industrial chain.

A retail energy tariff is typically reset from a wholesale reference over a
lagged window of several months. That means an autumn storage scare reaches the
tariff — and therefore the headline inflation print — in the first quarter of the
following year, arriving precisely when a central bank is deciding whether the
previous year's disinflation was durable.

This is why an energy story is so often really a rates story. The gas market
moves the CPI through a fixed lag, and the fixed lag lands the shock in the
middle of a policy meeting.

## What to watch

The **injection rate against the five-year band**, weekly, through the summer —
not the price. Storage that is filling slowly in July has already decided
something about February that the forward curve will not acknowledge until
October.

The specific level to watch is the end-of-injection fill. Above roughly ninety
per cent, a cold winter is absorbed by inventory and the price barely responds.
Below eighty, the same winter has to be rationed by price — and that is the
version that reaches the inflation print, and the rate decision after it.
